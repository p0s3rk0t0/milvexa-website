How your rewards work

Sign in to see the current reward amount and withdrawal threshold.

  1. Upload your charging receipt. We read the details automatically. Check them and add your receiving Solana wallet address.
  2. Rewards accumulate after approval. Each approved receipt earns the pilot reward shown in your account. Pending or rejected receipts do not add to your available balance.
  3. Request a combined payout. Once your available rewards for the same wallet exceed the withdrawal threshold shown in your account, select “Request rewards”.
  4. Receive MVXA in your wallet. Your payout request is processed after submission. The amount moves to “Payment in progress” and is marked “Paid” after the transfer is checked on Solana.

An available balance is a record of rewards owed to you; the tokens are not yet in your wallet. You need your own wallet that supports Solana tokens. Registration alone does not earn rewards, and MVXA has no guaranteed cash value.
