A clear rewards process.

Approval and payment are separate steps. Your pilot account shows the current reward, withdrawal threshold and claim status.

01

Charge & submit

Create an account, upload a charging receipt and check the extracted details. Add your receiving Solana wallet address.

02

We review the receipt

The team checks your claim before approval. Uploading a receipt does not guarantee a reward or a payment.

03

Accrue MVXA rewards

Approved rewards accumulate under the current pilot rules. Request a payout when your balance meets the withdrawal conditions shown in your account.

MVXA is a tradable Solana token. A token reward is not a fixed cash amount or a guaranteed return.

Before you start

A few useful answers

Are partner discounts already available?

No. Partner discounts are a proposal for a future agreed pilot. The programme has no announced active partner discount offers.

Does MVXA have a fixed value in euros?

No. MVXA is a tradable Solana token with a variable market price. Rewards are token amounts, not guaranteed cash returns or a guaranteed discount.

Does the pilot need a charger integration?

The current receipt submission pilot does not need a direct charger connection. Applying a future discount requires an agreed method supported by the operator, such as a promo code or a software integration.

What risks should participants understand?

MVXA is a speculative token. Low liquidity and concentrated holdings create risks. Verify the official mint and read the token risk disclosures before interacting. Pilot participation does not guarantee a profit.
