A 90-second illustrated mobile guide to creating a wallet and finding your public Solana address.

90 seconds · English on-screen instructions · Music · No spoken narration

Follow the setup, one step at a time.

  1. Download the official app. Start at phantom.com/download and choose your device.
  2. Choose the recovery phrase method. On the welcome screen, look for More Options → Create Recovery Phrase. Labels can differ by app version.
  3. Keep your recovery phrase offline. Write down the words in order. Never share them. Confirm the backup and complete your username and device protection prompts.
  4. Find your receiving address. From Home, open Deposit → Receive Crypto, select Solana and copy your main wallet's public address. Some versions show Receive directly.
For MVXA: use your main Solana wallet address.

Do not use a Cash account address. MILVEXA never needs your recovery phrase or private key. You do not connect a wallet or sign anything to watch this guide.
